What the architect can challenge

Identity and replay

Stable distribution keys; per-request acknowledged writes are skipped. Ambiguous acknowledgment requires reconciliation. No end-to-end exactly-once claim.

Control and evidence

The advisor never executes recovery. A different simulated actor approves a fingerprint-bound proposal. Stale evidence blocks execution. Real enterprise identity enforcement is future work.

State and recovery

SQLite atomically commits local request state, audit events and the Signal projection. Concurrent local execution is serialized. Production leases, fencing and external crash windows require sandbox proof.

Business completion

A job success is not delivery success. Channel acknowledgment is not consumer readiness. Green consumers require expected-content verification in the local receiver.
